S.A.G.E

ERP for purchasing, inventory and management

A business management system built for restaurants. At closing time, the team records what's left over; S.A.G.E works out how much of each item to order and sends the order to each supplier over the restaurant's own WhatsApp. Reports come out as PDFs in the system or straight on WhatsApp, for anyone allowed to request them.

  • Automatic purchase orders over WhatsApp, with a rule per item: weekly table, minimum and maximum stock, or recipe by production range
  • Inventory with receiving, stock in and out, waste tracking and weekly or monthly stock counts
  • Cost management: recipe costing with cost, food cost percentage and margin for each menu item
  • JasperReports reports on orders, inventory and KPIs, issued in the system or over WhatsApp
  • Multi-tenant, and I built the whole cycle: data modeling, REST API, interface and deployment on a Linux VPS with Nginx and HTTPS
Built for
Restaurants and small food businesses. In daily use at a burger restaurant in Pimenta Bueno, Brazil
Built with
Vue.js 3, Spring Boot, PostgreSQL, JasperReports, WhatsApp integration

AI customer service

Multi-tenant automated customer service platform

An assistant that answers customers over messaging: it looks up the catalog, checks stock and records orders, always with the database as the single source of truth. The AI runs on our own server, with no per-message cost and no data sent outside.

  • One installation serves several businesses, each with its own isolated catalog and settings
  • Admin panel with bcrypt-hashed passwords and sessions that expire on their own
  • Protection against AI manipulation (prompt injection) with filters and output validation
Built for
Businesses that serve and sell over WhatsApp
